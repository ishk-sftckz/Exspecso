# Record evidence method and evaluator separately

Each Acceptance Criterion declares credible proof using two dimensions: an Evidence Method of test, inspection, observation, or external confirmation, and an Evidence Evaluator of command, independent agent, or human. Deterministic behavior uses automated evidence when reasonably feasible, while human judgment is reserved for subjective, visual, physical, external, or policy-sensitive claims that require it. Agent-evaluated evidence records runtime and model provenance and cannot be presented as human verification. AI may prepare or analyze evidence for a human, but Human Attestation means the person reviewed the underlying result and made the final judgment. Missing or rejected required evidence blocks completion, an unverifiable Acceptance Criterion fails Readiness Validation, and Closure Verification cannot conceal missing criterion-level proof. V1 provides auditable attestation rather than cryptographic proof of human attention.
