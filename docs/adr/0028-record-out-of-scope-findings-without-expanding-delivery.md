# Record out-of-scope findings without expanding delivery

An issue discovered outside approved Task scope is recorded in that Task's append-only Out-of-scope Findings section with a stable finding ID, observation, evidence, risk, and suggested destination. It is not fixed or added to the Plan implicitly. A finding that does not affect current evidence is surfaced through Specification status and Final Review while delivery continues; one that invalidates a current assumption, dependency, or Acceptance Criterion blocks the Task pending `/exspecso-update` or human judgment. Promotion requires an approved Plan or Specification revision or a newly declared Specification. Status Summaries project unresolved findings, while explicit disposition with rationale closes or dismisses them without deleting history.
