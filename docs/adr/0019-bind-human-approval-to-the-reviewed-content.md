# Bind human approval to the reviewed content

Exspecso presents the material candidate content or diff together with a short fingerprint, and explicit human confirmation records the approved fingerprint, timestamp, and human actor declaration in the canonical artifact. Specification approval and Plan readiness may be confirmed together only when the approval covers both exact revisions and the included Task set; that Plan approval authorizes its Tasks without separate per-Task approval. Material edits require a new candidate revision and Revision Impact Record, so an earlier approval cannot silently apply to changed content. In-scope Correction Loop attempts remain authorized by the approved contract and budget. V1 provides an auditable content-bound workflow boundary rather than cryptographic identity authentication, excludes silent agent self-approval, and provides no default unattended approval bypass.
