# Classify drift before repair or invalidation

Exspecso classifies divergence before acting: projection drift affects generated summaries only; editorial drift preserves semantic fingerprints but requires explicit carry-forward; contract drift enters revision impact analysis; implementation drift invalidates evidence reachable through affected trace links; and structural or unclassifiable drift blocks mutation. `/exspecso-status` recomputes canonical state read-only, reports differences, and shows expected projections. Authorized mutations regenerate CLI-owned summaries, while a maintenance-only `exspecso repair-projections` command may repair them without product-state changes. Exspecso never overwrites externally edited canonical content automatically, and every repair records its rationale and before-and-after fingerprints.
