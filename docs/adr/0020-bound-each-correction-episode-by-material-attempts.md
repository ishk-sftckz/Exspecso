# Bound each Correction Episode by material attempts

A Correction Episode begins from one failed Task check, Closure Verification, or implementation-fix review finding and defaults to at most three implementation attempts. Assessment and diagnosis do not consume the budget; applying a material patch and rerunning the applicable Evidence Contract consumes one attempt. Each attempt records its hypothesis, changed areas, evidence result, and learning. Corrections remain inside approved implementation scope and cannot rewrite Requirements, Acceptance Criteria, or Verification Intent. Passing ends the episode and permits checkpointing; exhaustion leaves the Task `verification-failed`, stops implementation with `correction-exhausted`, and recommends human intervention. Projects may configure another positive limit without expanding scope, and later human-authorized work starts a new episode while preserving exhausted history.
