# 🧭 Exspecso Branding & Design Guidelines

> **“Spec-first delivery with progressive structural gravity, enforced through artifact truth and bounded agent autonomy.”**

The Exspecso brand is built on the core tension of its principles: balancing raw execution power (Speed/GSD energy) with disciplined structure (Safety/BMAD rigor). It combines the caffeinated jolt of an espresso with the engineered precision of a specification.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Voice
- **Tone:** Sharp, authoritative, pragmatic, and fast.
- **Vibe:** "Engineered momentum." We do not celebrate chaos, nor do we worship bureaucracy. We celebrate moving as fast as structurally possible.
- **Core Metaphors:**
  - **The Liquid (Execution/Speed):** Dynamic, fluid, fast-moving.
  - **The Cup/Grid (Structure/Safety):** Rigid, predictable, bounding the liquid so it doesn't spill.

## 2. Logo Concepts & Symbology
The logo must reflect the combination of **Coffee/Espresso** and **Spec-Driven Structure**.
- **Visual Elements to Combine:**
  - *Speed:* Steam lines, dynamic motion, espresso drops, fluid paths.
  - *Structure:* Blueprints, geometric grids, checkmarks, code brackets ( `{ }`, `[ ]`, `< >` ), bounding boxes.
- **Themes:**
  - *The Bounded Drop:* A fast-moving espresso drop caught inside a rigid geometric bracket.
  - *The Engineered Cup:* A minimalist coffee cup constructed entirely out of architectural lines and nodes.
  - *The Spec Bean:* A coffee bean divided by strict, deterministic grid lines.

## 3. Color Palette
The colors reflect the transition from raw energy to structured safety, grounded in a warm, earthy aesthetic inspired by specialty coffee.

* **Base / Background (The Roast)**
  - `Espresso Roast` (`#1E1511`): Deep, warm, earthy dark brown background. Relaxing and grounded.
* **Primary Accent (The Energy / Execution)**
  - `Caramel Crema` (`#D5A770`): Warm, inviting beige/caramel. Represents GSD energy, superpowers, and raw execution without the harshness of neon.
* **Secondary Accent (The Structure / Safety)**
  - `Mocha Line` (`#8C5A35`): A richer, muted earthy tone for structural elements. Represents tests passing, boundaries, specs, and deterministic safety gracefully integrated with the coffee theme.
* **Neutral Text (The Trace)**
  - `Latte Foam` (`#F4EEDD`) and `Muted Cocoa` (`#A38F85`): For readable, clear artifact documentation with a soft touch.

## 4. Typography
Exspecso requires a font stack that balances editorial readability with technical precision.

* **Primary Font (Headers & Branding):** `Inter` or `Geist` — Clean, modern, sans-serif. Represents "Progressive Complexity" (starts simple, scales well).
* **Secondary Font (Specs, Code, Traceability):** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` — Monospaced, deterministic. Used whenever "Artifact Truth" is displayed, such as system logs, traces, or spec boundaries.

## 5. UI/UX Design Principles
Derived directly from the Exspecso foundations:
* **Lazy UI (Progressive Complexity):** Interfaces should start overwhelmingly simple (Lite mode) and only reveal advanced controls (routing, RALP controls, merge safety) when the context demands it. No empty template forests.
* **Artifacts Over Chat:** The central focus of the UI should be the *document/artifact*, not the conversational feed. The chat is secondary; the spec is the source of truth.
* **Explicit Status:** Progress bars and statuses must be calculated directly from passing tests and spec verification, not inferred. Use the Verification Blue for "Done".
* **Bounded Correctness loops:** Visual indicators of autonomous loops should look like a tightly controlled orbit, not infinite loading spinners. Show the bounds (e.g., "Attempt 2/4").

## 6. Iconography
* **Specs/Requirements:** Checkmarks locked in brackets `[✓]`.
* **Execution:** A lightning bolt or fast-forward icon inside a coffee cup.
* **Traceability:** Solid, unbroken lines connecting nodes.
* **Context/Memory:** Hard drives or documents, explicitly refusing to show "brain/magic" icons to avoid implying unstructured hallucination.

---

*For generating visual assets, aim for dark backgrounds, sharp geometric lines, and a stark contrast between the fluid "espresso" colors and the rigid "spec" grids.*
